How it works: 4 risk domains

Our AI evaluates every project across four weighted domains to produce a balanced risk score.

Scope Creep

Assesses project complexity, number of stakeholders, contract type, and change order history. Higher complexity = higher risk.

Safety & Site Hazards

Evaluates work at height, confined spaces, hazardous materials, and neighboring structures. Flags high-risk site conditions.

Budget & Schedule Pressure

Measures budget contingency, timeline compression, penalty clauses, and resource availability. Tight margins increase the score.

Compliance & Regulatory

Reviews permit status, environmental regulations, zoning requirements, and inspection history. Gaps here raise immediate alerts.

Each domain contributes to a weighted total — the final 0–100 score tells you exactly where your project stands.

How it works: Score + Summary

Every assessment generates two powerful outputs — a clear score and an AI-written executive brief.

Risk Score: 0–100

Low (0–30): Standard monitoring sufficient
Medium (31–55): Targeted mitigation recommended
High (56–75): Active intervention required
Critical (76–100): Immediate escalation needed

AI Executive Summary (Llama 3.3 70B)

A natural-language breakdown of the key risk drivers, a plain-English explanation of the score, and 3–5 prioritized, actionable recommendations to reduce risk. Written in seconds, ready to share with stakeholders.

The combination of a quantitative score and a qualitative narrative gives you both the "what" and the "why" — so you can act with confidence.
