## PART 2 — Defense Read-Aloud

Good morning. My name is Honeybell, and I will be presenting the landing page of Sitewise — which is the first thing a user sees when they visit our platform.

The page opens with a white pill-shaped navigation bar fixed at the top. On the left is the Sitewise logo — a dark circle with the letters "SW." In the center are navigation links to different sections of the page, and on the right there is a live clock showing the current time in London alongside a prominent call-to-action button that reads "Start Free Assessment." When you hover over that button, the text rolls upward, which is a subtle animation effect we use throughout the page.

Beneath the navbar is the hero section. The background is not a static color — it is an animated canvas powered by a library called "shaders." This library runs four effects simultaneously: a swirling motion pattern, a flowing orange chroma effect, a glass distortion effect, and a very faint film grain overlay. Together they create a subtle, premium-looking texture that moves gently in the background. All of this runs in the browser using WebGL.

The hero content is positioned at the bottom of the viewport. There is a small label that reads "Sitewise," followed by the main headline: "Eliminate construction risk blind spots with AI-powered precision." Below the headline are two interactive elements. The first is an orange button that says "Start a project" — which also has the roll-up text animation and an arrow icon that rotates when hovered. The second is a white partner badge that displays "Powered by Groq AI" with a "Featured" tag. This badge is designed to build immediate trust with visitors.

Scrolling down, users encounter the features section. This is a two-by-two grid of four video cards. Each card contains an autoplay video, a button that expands from a small circle to a full pill when you hover over it, a short description, and a title. The four features covered are: the Risk Scoring Engine, AI Executive Summaries, Project History with PDF Reports, and Enterprise-Grade Security.

At the very bottom is a simple footer containing the Sitewise logo, navigation links, and copyright information.

In summary, the landing page is designed to immediately communicate Sitewise's value proposition, establish trust through visual polish and partner credentials, and guide visitors toward starting a free assessment. Everything from the shader background to the text animations is intentional — it creates a professional, modern feel that matches the quality of our product.
