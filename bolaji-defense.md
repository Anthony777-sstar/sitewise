## PART 2 — Defense Read-Aloud

Good morning. My name is Bolaji, and I will be demonstrating the Buying Assistant — an AI-powered chat feature built into Sitewise that helps users navigate property-related processes across different countries.

The core problem we are solving is straightforward. Property rules vary significantly from one country to another. The documents needed to buy land in Nigeria are completely different from what is required in the UK or the USA. Instead of spending hours searching the internet and hoping the information is accurate, users can simply ask our AI assistant and receive guidance based on official country-specific guides that we have loaded into the system.

When a user starts a new conversation, a popup appears asking them to make two selections. First, they choose their location — Nigeria, the UK, or the USA. Second, they choose their intent — whether they want to Buy a House, Build a House, or Buy Land. These two selections determine which set of official rules the AI will reference when answering questions.

Once these selections are made, the user enters the chat interface. On the left side is a sidebar that shows the Sitewise logo, a button to start a new conversation, and a list of past conversations that the user can return to at any time. In the center is the main chat area where the conversation takes place. User messages appear on the right side in orange-colored bubbles, and AI responses appear on the left side with an avatar next to them.

At the bottom of the chat area is the text input box. Users type their questions here and press send to receive answers from the AI. There is also a paperclip button next to the input box that allows users to upload PDF files. This is useful if a user has a land document, a property guide, or any other PDF — they can upload it and ask the AI specific questions about the contents of that document.

One feature worth highlighting is the bridge between the Buying Assistant and the risk assessment tool. When the AI provides advice, it can also suggest that the user run a formal risk assessment on their project. A green button labeled "Run Risk Assessment" appears inside the AI's reply. Clicking it takes the user directly to the Risk Scoring Engine, where they can get a quantified risk score for their project.

To give you a concrete idea of what users can ask: they might ask "What documents do I need to buy land in Lagos?" or "What are the building codes in Texas?" or "How much does it cost to register a property in the UK?" The AI answers these questions based on the official guides for the selected country.

In summary, the Buying Assistant acts like a property expert who knows the rules for each country. It saves users hours of research, provides accurate information, and connects directly to Sitewise's risk assessment feature for a complete workflow from guidance to evaluation.
