# Guide: Buying a House (USA)

RULES TO FOLLOW — Buying a House in USA
- Get pre-approved for a mortgage before you start house hunting so you know your budget.
- Hire a buyer's agent who represents your interests, not the seller's.
- Schedule a professional home inspection before committing to the purchase.
- Review all seller disclosure documents for known issues with the property.
- Budget for closing costs (typically 2–5% of the purchase price) plus property tax, homeowners insurance, and potential HOA fees.

RULES TO AVOID / RED FLAGS — Buying a House in USA
- Don't waive the inspection contingency unless you have expert-level knowledge of the property's condition.
- Be cautious of foundation cracks, outdated electrical systems, water damage in basements, and roof age — these are expensive to fix.
- Avoid properties with unpermitted renovations — they may not pass inspection or appraisal.
- Don't skip an appraisal contingency if you're financing — the bank won't lend more than the appraised value.

USEFUL LINKS — USA
- Property records in the US are county-level, not national — there is no single federal registry. Check with your specific county assessor or recorder's office for property records and title searches.
