# Guide: Buying Land (USA)

RULES TO FOLLOW — Buying Land in USA
- Research zoning regulations for the parcel before making an offer — zoning determines what you can build.
- Verify ownership through a title search and title insurance before closing.
- Commission a soil test to confirm the ground can support construction.
- Get a professional survey to confirm boundary lines and easements.
- Budget for survey, soil test, environmental assessment, and legal fees beyond the land price.

RULES TO AVOID / RED FLAGS — Buying Land in USA
- Don't buy land without verifying utility availability — some rural parcels have no water, power, or sewer access.
- Avoid wetlands-designated land without confirmed permits — building restrictions are severe.
- Be cautious of land with no legal road access — you cannot get a mortgage or building permit for landlocked parcels.
- Don't assume zoning allows your intended use — check with the local planning department before committing.

USEFUL LINKS — USA
- Property records in the US are county-level, not national — there is no single federal registry. Check with your specific county assessor or recorder's office for property records and title searches.
