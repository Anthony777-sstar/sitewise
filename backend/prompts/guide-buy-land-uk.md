# Guide: Buying Land (UK)

RULES TO FOLLOW — Buying Land in UK
- Check the local plan and development framework to understand what the land can be used for.
- Conduct a local authority search, environmental search, and title search via a solicitor or conveyancer.
- Commission a survey to assess ground conditions, access, and any contamination risks.
- Verify whether the land has existing planning permission or realistic potential for it.
- Budget for solicitor fees, survey, stamp duty, and planning application fees.

RULES TO AVOID / RED FLAGS — Buying Land in UK
- Don't assume land without planning permission can get it — green belt and Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty designations severely limit development.
- Be cautious of land with restricted access or no right of way — this affects mortgageability and build feasibility.
- Avoid contaminated land without confirmed remediation costs — cleanup can exceed the land value.
- Don't overlook restrictive covenants that may prevent building or limit what you can construct.
